# v4.5.5 - 2026-09-12

## ⚡ Performance

- **Idle grains retain about 54% less heap** ([#1358](https://github.com/Tochemey/goakt/pull/1358)). An activated, idle grain now holds about `728 B` of live heap instead of about `1,591 B`, and a long-lived grain (`WithLongLivedGrain`) about `555 B` instead of about `1,418 B`. A node keeping a million grains resident is correspondingly cheaper, and one million live grains under sustained load measure `609 B` each.

  | Measurement                                                | Before    | After     | Change |
  |------------------------------------------------------------|-----------|-----------|--------|
  | Live heap per idle grain                                   | `1,591 B` | `728 B`   | `-54%` |
  | Live heap per idle long-lived grain (`WithLongLivedGrain`) | `1,418 B` | `555 B`   | `-61%` |
  | Grain process struct                                       | `240 B`   | `192 B`   | `-20%` |
  | Live heap for one million idle grains on one node          | `1.59 GB` | `0.73 GB` | `-54%` |

  The reduction comes from seven changes to the per-grain layout:

  - The default user mailbox is embedded in the grain process instead of allocated as a separate heap object with its own sentinel; grains configured with a mailbox capacity keep their standalone bounded mailbox.
  - The async response queue is attached only when the grain is configured for reentrancy, at activation or at runtime, instead of allocated for every grain.
  - The timer registry is created by the first timer scheduled in an activation and dropped when the activation ends, so a grain that never schedules a timer holds none.
  - Per-grain copies of the remoting client, the logger and a dedicated shutdown channel are gone; the logger is looked up from the actor system on demand and shutdown is acknowledged through the poison pill's own channel.
  - The identity keeps one string, its `kind/name` form, with the kind and the name as views into it instead of three separate copies.
  - Activation settings are stored as plain fields, and the dependencies map exists only when `WithGrainDependencies` is used.
  - The process struct is laid out on three whole cache lines, so the fields producers read on every send never share a line with the fields the processing turn writes.

  Grain semantics are unchanged: one message at a time, send order preserved, `TellGrain` returns when the grain has processed the message, `AskGrain` returns the reply, and timeouts behave as before. Throughput is unchanged.

- **`AskGrain` is allocation-free in steady state** ([#1358](https://github.com/Tochemey/goakt/pull/1358)). The reply channel of a grain ask is now borrowed from a per-shard pool and returned once the reply is in hand, the same discipline `TellGrain` already applies to its acknowledgment channel; a request that times out or is cancelled abandons its channel, so a late reply can never reach a reused one. Each `AskGrain` previously allocated `128 B` in two objects for its reply channel; it now allocates nothing. On an 8-thread Apple M1 the aggregate rate across eight caller/grain pairs moved from about `2.5M` to about `2.9M` messages per second, and the single-caller rate is unchanged within noise.

## 🔧 Fixes

- **`WithRelocationDisabled` survives shutdown and restart** ([#1349](https://github.com/Tochemey/goakt/issues/1349)). The actor teardown no longer resets the relocation and singleton flags, and stopped actors are excluded from the graceful-shutdown relocation snapshot.

- **A failed remote grain activation no longer displaces the owner** ([#1350](https://github.com/Tochemey/goakt/issues/1350)). The owner's registry entry is released only after a transport failure and once cluster membership confirms the node has left; every other error is returned to the caller. A claim made for a remote peer is rolled back when the peer rejects the activation and kept when the outcome is unknown. Every release of a registry entry is conditional on the entry still naming the expected node, with the check and the deletion serialized cluster-wide, and a failed rollback of a claim is reported instead of discarded.
